      Reading the numbers

      Account creation and page tracking are separate systems. A new account may have used another app version or device, and some page events may fail to arrive.

      “Reached” counts unique app installs per page. “Continued” counts installs with a recorded primary action on that page. Returning to a page can affect daily totals.
