# Speaking Coach privacy policy: analytics

This is the finalized analytics section for the Speaking Coach privacy policy. It is maintained here for inclusion in the public policy.

## Product analytics information we collect

Speaking Coach records product-usage events to understand how people use the app and improve it. Events include the screen or onboarding step opened, whether you continued or left it, time spent on a screen, and selections from fixed-choice onboarding questions. Those selections can include your language, speaking category and situation, timing, readiness rating from 0 to 10, ratings of predefined speaking challenges, selected costs, and desired outcomes.

First-party analytics records sent to our backend may include a randomly generated app-install identifier, a visit identifier, the event time, the relevant screen or step number, and, if you are signed in, your account identifier. When PostHog analytics is enabled in the app build, PostHog receives page and onboarding-choice events, a visit identifier, its SDK's anonymous identifier, and standard app and device information such as app version, device type, and operating-system version. PostHog also receives the IP address used to send the request and may use it to estimate your general location.

Analytics events do not include your name, free-text onboarding responses, or the words you speak during a practice session. We use these events for product analytics, not for advertising or cross-company tracking.

## How we use analytics information

We use product-usage information to measure onboarding and feature engagement, understand where people stop or continue, and improve Speaking Coach. We use fixed-choice onboarding responses to understand which practice needs and goals the app should support.

## Analytics service providers

Supabase receives and stores first-party app page, action, timing, and step events. When PostHog is enabled in the app build, PostHog receives the page and onboarding-choice events described above. These providers process event data and related identifiers on our behalf to provide analytics services and help us understand product usage. The app is configured to send PostHog events to its US service endpoint. We do not send names, free-text responses, or practice speech to these providers as analytics event properties.
