**Description**
```
Practice the conversations that matter, out loud, before they happen. Speaking Coach gives you an AI partner who plays the other side: your interviewer, your audience, your manager, or a stranger who says hi.

Pick the moment. Speak. Get feedback on what you actually said. Then retry the exact moment that tripped you up.

AI MOCK INTERVIEWS
• Add your CV, the job post or your notes. Your interviewer reads them and asks about your real experience
• A hiring manager who listens and follows up on your answers
• Tell me about yourself, explain your experience with an example, think under pressure
• A 5-session plan for your interview day, with a reminder the day before

PRESENTATION PRACTICE
• Add your slides as a PDF and practice your talk out loud
• Swipe through your slides like a clicker while you speak
• Then answer the questions your audience would ask

SMALL TALK TRAINING
• A stranger says hi. Keep it going, without freezing
• Meet someone new, ask for a raise, set a boundary
• Any situation: describe the conversation and your partner plays the other side

INSTANT FEEDBACK ON YOUR WORDS
• Every note quotes something you actually said
• How you did, what to keep, and one change to try
• Retry this moment: 90 seconds on the same question, then see what changed
• We never rate your accent or your personality

BUILD THE HABIT
• A daily 30-second speaking prompt
• A streak, and a history of every session

PRACTICE IN YOUR LANGUAGE
The screens, the voice partner and the feedback work in 20 languages, including Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Hindi, Arabic, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

Set the pressure to Gentle, Realistic or Tough. Pause any time with "I need a moment", or tap "Help me" for a sentence to start from.

Your CV and documents stay on your phone. Only their text goes to your AI partner during interview sessions.

SUBSCRIPTION
Speaking Coach needs a subscription, monthly or yearly. New subscribers may get a free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings.

Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
```

## Before you submit

- **EU availability.** The app isn't live in any of the 27 EU countries. Set your Digital Services Act trader status under Business in App Store Connect, then make sure the EU countries are ticked in Pricing and Availability.
- **App Privacy.** Interview documents send their text to ElevenLabs as part of the voice partner's instructions. Check that the App Privacy answers and the privacy policy cover content the user adds, not just what they say.
- **Ratings.** The app never asks for a rating. Add `AppStore.requestReview` after a clear win, such as a retry that shows "Clearer this time." or the third finished session.
